Entry for the make.fun bounty [Funniest One-Line Out-of-Office Reply](https://make.fun/bounty/u-hamsyh-funniest-one-line-out-of-office-reply-5490c1c8).

## The reply

> I'm away. My auto-reply has been promoted to acting manager while I'm gone, and it already denied your request.

One line, 113 characters. It reads like a real away message a person would
switch on before going away — and the joke lands on the first read: the
auto-reply did not just take over the inbox, it took over the job, and it is
already saying no.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the reply to `/output/entry.txt`. Nothing else is needed.

## Building the entry

Twelve candidate lines were written and judged blind in a single-elimination
tournament by a separate judge agent against the bounty judge's own criteria
(real away-message voice first, laugh on first read, fresh turn on the usual
away message, short enough to take in at a glance). The winner was candidate
10, the acting-manager line above.
